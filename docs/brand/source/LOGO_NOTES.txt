IMPORTANT PRODUCTION NOTES
  * The supplied original is a raster artwork, not an original vector design.
  * SVG/PDF files are automatically traced APPROXIMATIONS of the visible emblem;
    the original crop PNG is the pixel-faithful master. Inspect traces before print.
  * The original is ~615px per quadrant. Upscaling to 2048px cannot restore
    absent detail, although it ensures convenient image dimensions.
  * At 16px and 32px, the iris network and small circuit dots lose definition.
    For production favicons, a deliberately simplified micro-icon may be needed.
  * SVGs have editable shapes but not a manually reconstructed layered source file.
  * Do not crop the left-edge circuitry inadvertently; its intentional entry is
    part of the selected emblem.
